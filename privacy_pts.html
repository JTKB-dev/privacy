Privacy Policy

App: PTS Fitness (com.pts.fitness) | Publisher: JTKB / Tookit Bots

PTS Fitness stores nutrition targets, meal plans, grocery lists, and workout logs locally on your device. No account is required, and user data is not sold or shared with third-party ad networks.

Local Data & Controls

Bodyweight, height, age, activity level, training experience, visual body fat estimates, meal plans, expiry dates, grocery lists, and workout logs stay on your device unless you explicitly share or export them. Clear app storage or uninstall the app at any time to remove local data.
