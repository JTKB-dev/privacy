PTS – Personal Training Services – Privacy Policy

App: PTS – Personal Training Services (Android package com.pts.fitness)
Developer: JTKB
Effective date: 21 November 2026

Summary

PTS keeps your fitness and nutrition information on your device. There are no PTS accounts and no PTS servers, and the developer does not receive, sell or share your personal data. The only outside services are Google Play (for subscriptions) and Google's ML Kit libraries (for on-device scanning), described below.

Information stored on your device

  • Profile and body data you enter, such as age, height, weight, estimated body-fat percentage, goals (for example maintain, cut, lean bulk or regain) and weekly check-ins. This is health and fitness information. It is stored only in the app's private storage on your phone.
  • Training and nutrition records: workouts, rest timers, meals, macro targets, pantry items, shopping lists and reminders.
  • Settings: for example reminder times and an optional workout-music link.
  • Photos you take or choose for pantry, food or receipt scanning. These are processed on the device. Temporary image files stay in the app's private cache.

Device and cloud backup are turned off for PTS, so this data is not copied to Google backups. You can make your own backup with the in-app export (JSON, CSV or Markdown). You choose where that file is saved and who you share it with. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes everything stored by PTS on the device.

Camera and on-device scanning (Google ML Kit)

If you allow camera access, PTS uses Google ML Kit's on-device features (image labelling, text recognition and barcode scanning) to read pantry items, food labels and receipts. Images are processed on your device and are not uploaded by PTS. As part of Google's ML Kit software, Google may collect limited technical information, such as device and app information and API performance and diagnostics, to maintain and improve ML Kit. This is governed by Google's terms and privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy).

Subscriptions (Google Play Billing)

Paid features are sold through Google Play Billing. Google Play processes the payment. PTS and the developer never see your card or payment details. The app receives only the purchase status from Google Play (for example product, purchase token and whether it is active and acknowledged) to unlock membership on your device. Google's handling of payments is covered by the Google Play terms and Google's privacy policy.

App integrity checks

To protect against tampered copies, PTS checks on your device that it was signed by the developer and installed from Google Play. These checks do not send your personal data to the developer.

Notifications and other permissions

  • Notifications, vibration and alarms after restart: used for workout reminders and the rest timer, which are scheduled locally on your device.
  • Camera: used only when you choose to scan.
  • Internet / network state: used by Google Play Billing and Google's libraries. PTS itself does not upload your data.

Links and sharing

If you tap a link (for example your workout-music link or this policy), it opens in your browser or another app, which has its own privacy policy. If you share a shopping list, it goes only to the app and person you pick.

Data sharing and sale

The developer does not sell, rent or share your personal data with anyone, and does not use it for advertising. PTS contains no advertising or analytics SDKs.

Health information

PTS gives general fitness and nutrition guidance. It is not medical advice. Your health information stays on your device unless you export or share it yourself.

Children

PTS is not directed at children under 13.

Your choices

  • Deny or revoke the camera or notification permissions in Android settings at any time. The rest of the app keeps working.
  • Delete your data with Reset Profile in the app, by clearing the app's storage, or by uninstalling.
  • Cancel a subscription in the Google Play Store under Payments & subscriptions.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.

Contact
