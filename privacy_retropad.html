Apex Deck – Privacy Policy

App: Apex Deck (Android package com.x5launcher)
Developer: JTKB
Effective date: 21 November 2026

Summary

Apex Deck does not collect, sell or share personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no crash-reporting services, and it does not send any information to the developer. Everything the app stores stays on your device.

What the app stores on your device

  • Your game library list: file names, sizes and locations of the game files in the folders you choose, plus favourites, recently played and hidden entries.
  • Folder permissions: the folders you pick with Android's system folder picker. The app can read only those folders.
  • Settings: controller profile, Demo mode, and the emulator you chose for each game.
  • Optional home-server settings: if you set up a home server, the app stores its address, share name, and SMB username and password on your device.
  • Optional cache: game files you choose to download from your own home server are cached in the app's private storage.

Device backup is turned off for this app, so this data is not copied to cloud backups. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes all of it.

Network use

The app connects to the network only when you configure the optional home-server feature. It then talks directly to the server address you entered (over SMB or HTTPS), usually a computer on your own home network, to list and download your own files. Your SMB username and password are sent only to that server, to log in. No data is sent to the developer or to any third party.

Permissions

  • Internet: only for the optional home-server feature described above.
  • Modify system settings (optional): if you allow it, the app temporarily locks screen rotation while an emulator runs, and restores your previous setting when you return.
  • Package visibility: the app checks whether specific emulator apps are installed, so it can open your games in them.

Other apps

When you start a game, the file is handed to the emulator app you installed separately. That app has its own privacy policy.

No games included

Apex Deck contains no games, ROMs, BIOS files or third-party artwork, and it does not download any from the internet. You supply your own legally owned files.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions or requests: reply to the message that sent you the app, or use the developer contact on the app's store listing.
