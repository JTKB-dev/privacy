Apex Deck

A controller-first home screen that turns your phone and a USB-C controller into a handheld console. It opens game apps (emulators) you already have on your phone. It doesn't come with any games.

  • Reverse-landscape lock: built for USB-C telescopic controllers, where normal auto-rotate fails or flips the screen upside down.
  • One-tap controller setup: Set controller presets writes rotation and button maps into your installed emulators, so HD upscaling and widescreen settings in those emulators work with your pad straight away.
  • Plug-and-play navigation: the whole menu works from the controller, with no touchscreen needed.

Demo Mode

No games or emulators? Open Settings in the launcher and switch on Demo / Store-Safe Mode. It shows three built-in sample titles (OpenArena Demo, 2048 Homebrew, Space Shooter PD) with generated art, so you can try the menus and controller navigation. Your games folder isn't scanned and nothing is deleted. Turn it off to see your own library again.

Have your own games? Put them in a folder called Games on your phone, tap Allow when the app asks for file access, plug in your controller, then tap Set controller presets.

Install in 3 steps (Android)

  1. Download the APK with the button above.
  2. Tap the file when the download finishes (or find it in your Downloads or Files app).
  3. If Android says it's blocked, tap Settings and turn on Install unknown apps (also called Allow from this source) for your browser or Files app, then go back and tap Install.

If Play Protect shows a warning, that's normal for test builds not yet on Google Play; tap More details then Install anyway. When the apps reach Google Play later, uninstall these test versions first, then install from the Play Store.
