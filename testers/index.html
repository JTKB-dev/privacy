Install in 3 steps (Google Play)

  1. Tap the green Join PTS Fitness Closed Testing on Google Play button above and sign in with your Google account.

  3. Tap the Google Play link on the next screen to install PTS Fitness directly from the Play Store.

Keep PTS Fitness installed for at least 14 days so your test counts toward the Google Play launch. If you installed an older APK version earlier, uninstall it first before installing from Google Play.
