PTS Fitness Closed Beta

Offline-first macro & calorie calculator, meal planner, food freshness tracker, and workout logger for Android.

How to Join

  1. Email your Google Play account address to tookitbots@gmail.com to be added to the tester list.
  2. Once confirmed, open the Google Play opt-in link provided in your reply and tap Become a tester.
  3. Install PTS Fitness from Google Play and keep it installed for at least 14 days during active testing.

What to Test

  • Calorie and macro targets using bodyweight, training experience, and visual body fat estimate
  • Meal planning, pantry/fridge expiry alerts, and grocery list sharing
  • Workout logging across home or gym sessions
